# depart_fling: the other way out (M2.3c's exit variant, paired with
# depart.txt the way wake_slide.txt is paired with wake.txt). Where depart
# gives up and sinks, this one *leaves*: it crouches, loads itself against the
# floor and fires off the top-right corner as a stretched streak, which is the
# same road wake_slide came in on, travelled the other way and upward. 12 fps,
# loop=false, 0.75 s -- shorter again than depart's second, because a decision
# to go is faster than a body giving way.
#
# The contract is depart.txt's and it is stated there in full: first frame is
# idle.txt's first frame cell for cell, last frame is empty. Everything
# between is this clip's own business, and here that is two frames of
# anticipation and three of nothing but the tail.
#
# No face past the crouch, for wake_slide's reason: eyes on a frame the body
# crosses in a twelfth of a second read as a drawing being dragged rather than
# as a body moving.
