# child_depart_fling: the other way a worker gets sent off (the second variant
# behind the `child_depart` trigger -- not M2.3c's `depart`, which is the
# avatar's own exit). Where child_depart.txt buds slowly and watches the
# child drift away, this one is a *throw*: the slime squats, snaps upright and
# the child is off the top of the grid in two frames, and then the body recoils
# from its own effort. 16 fps, loop=false, 0.56 s against child_depart's 1.0.
#
# The two have to differ in shape, not just in speed -- that is the lesson
# wake_slide was drawn to -- so one of them is a deliberate act and the other
# is something the body does. Spawn several tasks in a session and they
# alternate, which is the whole reason the trigger exists.
#
# There is no face on the flick frame. A body moving that fast is a shape, and
# eyes on it read as a drawing being dragged rather than as motion; wake_slide
# withholds them for the same three frames and the same reason.
#
# Ends on idle.txt's first frame, as every one-shot here does.
