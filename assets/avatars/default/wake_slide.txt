# wake_slide: the other way in (M7.1's first variant). Where wake.txt gathers
# itself up out of a puddle on the spot, this one *arrives* -- it slides in
# from the left, skids flat across the floor, piles up against its own
# momentum on the right and sloshes back. 10 fps, loop=false, and it is
# deliberately shorter than wake.txt (0.8 s against 1.9 s): the two entrances
# have to differ in shape, and one of them being brisk is half of that.
#
# The last frame is idle.txt's first frame, cell for cell, exactly as
# wake.txt's is. A one-shot holds its last drawing until something asks for
# another clip, so any entrance that ends anywhere else would leave the slime
# resting in a pose the breathe does not start from, and the handover would
# read as a jump. That is the contract between variants of one trigger: they
# may take any road they like, they must arrive at the same place.
#
# There is no face until the fourth frame. A blur coming through the door has
# no eyes in it; putting them on the fast frames makes the motion read as a
# drawing being dragged rather than as a body moving.
