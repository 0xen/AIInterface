# depart: the going-away, and the mirror of wake.txt. The slime shuts its
# eyes, lets go of its own shape, settles back into the puddle it got up out
# of, and the puddle thins until there is nothing left. 10 fps, loop=false,
# about a second -- brisker than wake's 1.9 s on purpose: getting up is an
# effort and giving up is not.
#
# **The exit contract, which is the entrance contract read backwards.** A
# one-shot entrance must *end* on idle.txt's first frame, because the loop
# that follows it starts there. An exit has the same obligation at both ends
# and they are different obligations:
#
#   * its FIRST frame is idle.txt's first frame, cell for cell -- the pose the
#     ambient clips are drawn around, so cutting to the exit from whatever was
#     on screen is a change of pose no larger than one tick of idle, and the
#     departure begins on a drawing instead of on a jump.
#   * its LAST frame is EMPTY. That is the half that carries weight. A
#     one-shot holds its last drawing until something asks for another clip,
#     so an exit that ended on any body at all would leave that body sitting
#     in the band waiting for an alpha fade to erase it -- which is the
#     dissolve this clip exists to replace. Ending on nothing means the
#     animation, and not the compositor, is what makes the avatar absent.
#
# Anything after the first frame is free: variants of an exit owe each other
# only those two frames, and the road between them should not look alike.
